This Week's 5 Requests

1

Say Hello, Team!

Ask 3 to 5 people to each say their first name, their job, and one sentence about what they do.

How to film: Hold your phone up and down (like a phone call), 3 to 5 seconds per person, at their actual desk or workbench.
2

The Milling Machine at Work

Film the mill cutting a crown or model, then the finished piece coming out.

How to film: Phone up and down, 10 to 15 seconds, get close to the machine window.

▶ Watch a real example first
3

Checking the Fit Under the Light

Hold a finished (not a real patient's) crown up to the inspection lamp. Get close on the edge where it meets the tooth.

How to film: Phone up and down, a photo or a 10 second video is fine.
4

Since 1951

Take a photo of any old lab photo, award, or sign we have. Then a photo of the lab today from a similar angle.

How to film: Two regular photos, no video needed.
5

A Design on the Screen

Film a technician's computer screen showing a tooth design, with their hand on the mouse.

How to film: Phone up and down, 10 to 15 seconds, hold steady.
!
 No patient information, ever. No patient names, faces, or anything with a name or case number visible on paper or on a screen.
